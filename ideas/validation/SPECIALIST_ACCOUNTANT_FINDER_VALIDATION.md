# Specialist Accountant Finder for Online Earners - 48h Validation

*Status: PROBATION. Goal: prove whether this is a buyer-helper gap or just accountant SEO content.*

---

## What To Test

Will Greeks with nonstandard online income/assets pay for a verified accountant shortlist and prep pack, instead of asking Reddit and guessing?

Target cases:
- Amazon KDP / Amazon seller income.
- IBKR, DEGIRO, Revolut, ETFs, US stocks.
- YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, OnlyFans, affiliates, sponsorships.
- Foreign clients, Stripe, PayPal, Upwork/Fiverr.

## Pass/Fail Gate

Pass only if, within 48 hours:
- 20 relevant people are contacted or replied to.
- 3+ people ask for details or send their case type.
- 5+ accountant offices explicitly confirm they handle at least one niche.
- 1+ person says EUR29-EUR49 is reasonable for the shortlist/prep pack.

If buyers engage but accountants cannot be verified, kill or reframe as content. If accountants verify but buyers do not engage, kill paid finder and keep the learning as a directory lead.

## Lead Surfaces

- r/PersonalFinanceGreece posts about Amazon KDP accountant, IBKR/DEGIRO/Revolut tax questions, ETF reporting and foreign-client freelancer setup.
- r/greece posts about internet income, platform income, freelancing and side hustles.
- Instagram/TikTok creator-tax comments only when a user asks for "who knows this?" not generic tax fear.
- Facebook groups for freelancers, e-shop owners, KDP/self-publishing and creators.

## Accountant Verification Questions

1. Do you currently handle clients with [Amazon KDP / IBKR / creator income / Stripe-PayPal foreign clients]?
2. What documents do you ask for before deciding if you can take the case?
3. Do you offer a first paid consultation or only ongoing monthly accounting?
4. What is the starting monthly fee or consultation fee?
5. Can I list you as accepting this niche if I send a prepared case summary?

## Safety Rules

- Do not give tax, legal or investment advice.
- Do not ask for Taxisnet, passwords, full tax returns, bank screenshots, ID, or sensitive documents.
- Ask only for generic case type, platform, country of client/platform, annual range band, and whether they already have accountant.
- Make clear the accountant is the professional responsible for advice and filing.

## Decision After Test

- 3+ buyers interested, 5+ accountants verified, 1+ price-positive: keep PROBATION and run a paid pilot.
- Buyers interested but fewer than 5 accountants verified: kill finder until supply bench exists.
- Accountants verified but buyers uninterested: kill paid service, keep as free SEO/directory idea only.
