# 2026-09-06 R8 Validation — Greek Native Remote Task Stack

## Candidate
Greek Native Remote Task Stack: a zero-capital, laptop-only supplier stack across Greek transcription, audio annotation, QA, voice recording and translation tasks.

## Why This Exists
Prior runs were right to kill Greek genealogy transcription as a consumer idea: old one-off posts and free/professional substitutes were too weak. The stronger pattern is not genealogy. It is current remote buyers needing Greek-native labor across several task shapes.

## Evidence
- GoTranscript advertises Greek transcription contractor access, worldwide/Greece eligibility, testing, no minimum hours and rates around USD0.50-1.20 per audio minute. This proves an application path, not income.
- Upwork search surfaced recent Greek transcription demand, including fixed-price Greek voice-file transcription and long-term Greek transcription projects in August 2026, plus a recent Greek audio annotation/transcription post.
- Appen/CrowdGen-style Greek specialist snippets advertise remote Greek transcription/annotation work, flexible hours and per-valid-audio-hour pay.
- Facebook-visible remote hiring snippets advertise freelance Greek transcribers with assessment and transcription rates.
- TranscribeMe/general transcription pages prove broader remote task-market existence, but are not Greek-specific enough to carry the score alone.

## Scoring
| Dimension | Score | Reason |
|---|---:|---|
| Capital zero | 9 | Laptop-only; no inventory, office or ads required for the first test. |
| Greece fit | 7 | Greek-native skill is the edge, although many buyers are global platforms rather than Greek local buyers. |
| Speed | 6 | Applications/bids can start today, but approval and response delays are real. |
| Market pull | 7 | Multiple current buyer/channel surfaces, including recent Upwork tasks and contractor pages. |
| Gap | 5 | Crowded with platforms and freelancers; edge is execution, availability and Greek accuracy, not defensibility. |
| Timing | 6 | Current September/August 2026 snippets plus AI/audio-data demand support near-term activity. |
| Solo fit | 9 | One person can test and deliver the first work. |

Raw average: 7.0. Strict cap: 6.6 PROBATION because platform acceptance, live queue and actual labor-hour economics are not proven.

## Validation Gate
1. Run a EUR0 timed Greek transcription sample: 5 minutes of audio, record actual time, error rate and fatigue.
2. Verify one accepted application/bid path: GoTranscript, Upwork or Appen/CrowdGen-style role.
3. Confirm live work availability or buyer response after acceptance/bid.
4. Calculate effective hourly yield after QA, not advertised audio-minute pay.

## Kill Conditions
- No acceptance or reachable buyer after the first application/bid batch.
- No live queue/tasks after acceptance.
- Effective hourly yield below EUR5 after measured transcription time and corrections.
- Work requires credentials, location or equipment the founder does not have.

## Learning
Platform presence is not automatically bad. A platform can be the buyer/channel for a first-cash experiment. But a contractor listing is not a business until access, queue and labor-hour yield are measured.
