# Student Housing Parent Shortlist - 48h Validation

*Status: PROBATION. Goal: prove whether parents/students will pay for judgement and scam-screening, not access to listings.*

---

## What To Test

Do Greek parents or students want a transparent housing shortlist that explains area safety, commute practicality, rent sanity and scam red flags, or do they only want free listings from Facebook/Spitogatos/agents?

## Pass/Fail Gate

Pass only if, within 48 hours:
- 20 relevant people are contacted or replied to.
- 5+ people ask for the sample checklist/report or give city/budget details.
- 3+ people explicitly say they would pay EUR25-EUR49 for route/area/scam-screened judgement.

If people only ask for listings, do not sell. Reframe as free content or kill the paid shortlist.

## Lead Surfaces

- Facebook housing groups where students/parents post "ψάχνω σπίτι", city, budget, school or neighborhood.
- Posts from parents asking whether an area is safe, convenient, close to university, or realistic by bus/metro.
- Scam-warning threads about fake agents, deposits before viewing, duplicate photos, "paid list" promises, or pressure to send money fast.
- Student-housing comment threads before/after university results and the September move-in rush.

## Offer Boundary

This is not brokerage and not paid access to secret listings.

The offer is a judgement layer:
- 8-12 public listings found from normal sources.
- Area and commute notes.
- Rent sanity check.
- Scam/red-flag checklist.
- Message template for contacting landlords/agents.
- A short "best 3 to contact first" ranking.

Θέλω να δω αν αυτό είναι πιο χρήσιμο από το να σας στείλει κάποιος απλώς links. Αν σας βοηθούσε, θα πληρώνατε 25-49 ευρώ για πλήρη shortlist;

### Scam Screen
- Does the same photo appear in many listings?
- Is the price far below similar listings?
- Does the owner ask for deposit before viewing?
- Is the profile new or anonymous?
- Are photos too polished or mismatched?

## Safety Rules

- Never ask for deposits or handle money.
- Never promise a booking or legal guarantee.
- Never sell "secret" listings.
- Avoid giving crime/safety certainty; phrase as practical parent/student notes from public info.
- If a listing looks suspicious, advise direct viewing and no deposit before verification.

## Decision After Test

- 5+ people request sample and 3+ paid-intent replies: keep PROBATION and build one paid pilot.
- People like it but will not pay: turn into free lead magnet/content, not a paid idea.
- Mostly "send listings" with no judgement demand: kill the paid shortlist.
