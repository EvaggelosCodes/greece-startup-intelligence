## Attack the strongest lead
If contractor transcription is a trap, the trap is quoting audio-hour revenue as working-hour income. USD0.50–1.20 per minute means USD30–72 per audio hour, **not per working hour**. Illustrative 4–6 working hours per audio hour gives USD5–18 gross per working hour before unpaid screening, waiting, corrections, fees and tax; neither speed nor queue is observed. Greek/global competition includes the platform workforce; the platform can be a customer rather than an exclusionary competitor. Actual task demand and founder delivery skill remain unverified. It fails the Monday-cash founder test for now. Genealogy stays killed at 6.1 with no new accepted-scope evidence; do not recycle it as a fourth candidate.

## A concrete EUR0 validation task, not an executed experiment
Use a self-recorded five-minute Greek passage with a known reference text; make no client-data or copyright assumptions. Transcribe it without looking at the reference. Log audio duration, actual work minutes, material omissions/errors, correction minutes and guideline compliance. Calculate audio-minute rate times five divided by total work hours. This is a controlled skill check, not proof of real-audio performance. If accurate, a future user-authorized application can test access; only accepted work tests payment and volume. Do not create an account or submit a sample automatically.
