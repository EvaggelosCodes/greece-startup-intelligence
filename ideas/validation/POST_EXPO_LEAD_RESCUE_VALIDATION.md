# 48h Post-Expo Lead Rescue - Validation

*Status: PROBATION. Goal: prove whether small Greek exhibitors pay for post-event execution, not generic export consulting.*

---

## What To Test

Will a small Greek exhibitor who has just attended or is about to attend an international trade fair accept help cleaning, prioritizing and following up their expo leads within 48 hours?

## Pass/Fail Gate

Pass only if, within 48 hours:
- 20 current Greek exhibitors are found from September exhibition lists, LinkedIn posts, chamber announcements, Enterprise Greece mentions, or exhibitor catalogues.
- 3+ reply to a direct message or email.
- 1+ sends a sample lead list, business-card photo, WhatsApp thread, or messy follow-up notes for a free mini-cleanup.
- 1+ says EUR79-EUR149 would be reasonable for a full cleanup/follow-up pack after seeing the sample.

Fail if replies say agencies already handle it, if no one will share even a non-sensitive sample, or if the task requires export strategy rather than admin execution.

## Offer Boundary

Promise:
- Clean a messy lead list from business cards, badge scans, WhatsApp notes, emails or spreadsheet exports.
- Tag leads by priority and next action.
- Draft Greek/English follow-up emails and short LinkedIn/WhatsApp messages.
- Build a 7-day follow-up checklist.

Non-promise:
- Do not promise new leads.
- Do not negotiate deals.
- Do not act as an export consultant.
- Do not send messages from the client's accounts unless they explicitly ask after trust exists.

## Target Sources

- Enterprise Greece September exhibition pages.
- Official exhibitor directories for September fairs.
- Chamber of commerce announcements.
- LinkedIn posts from Greek brands saying they are attending or just attended a fair.
- Exhibitor catalogues from food, cosmetics, materials, tourism supply, maritime, and manufacturing fairs.
- Small brands with no obvious export/agency team.
