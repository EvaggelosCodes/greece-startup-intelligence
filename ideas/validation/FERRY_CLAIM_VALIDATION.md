# Ferry Passenger Claim Pack - 48h Validation

*Status: PROBATION. Goal: prove whether ferry passengers will engage with and pay for claim-prep help, not legal representation.*

---

## What To Test

Do ferry passengers with delay, cancellation, refund refusal or lost-luggage pain want a simple Greek/English claim-prep helper, or will they use free official forms, consumer bodies, booking platforms or existing claim services?

## Pass/Fail Gate

Pass only if, within 48 hours:
- 20 recent relevant complaints are found and replied to or DM'd.
- 3+ people accept a free eligibility/checklist review.
- 1+ person says they would pay EUR15-EUR35 for a prepared claim pack or higher-value baggage/refund case support.

If people only want free advice, keep it as content, not a paid service. If lead density is too low again, kill the idea.

## Lead Surfaces

- Tripadvisor, Trustpilot, Reddit, Facebook and travel forums with recent SeaJets, Blue Star, Minoan or Greek ferry refund/delay/lost-luggage complaints.
- Comments where passengers mention delay length, cancelled ferry, refund refusal, luggage/baggage issue, missed connection, or "no answer" from company.
- Greek and English posts from tourists who likely do not want to navigate Greek complaint forms.

## Offer Boundary

This is not legal representation and not a guaranteed compensation service.

The offer is a claim-prep layer:
- Eligibility sanity check based on passenger's story.
- Checklist of documents/screenshots needed.
- Draft email/complaint text in Greek/English.
- Operator contact path and official complaint route.
- Optional follow-up reminder schedule.

## Claim Pack Template

### Case Summary
- Ferry company:
- Route:
- Travel date:
- Issue type: delay / cancellation / refund refusal / luggage / other
- Delay duration or amount disputed:
- Company response so far:

### Evidence Checklist
- Ticket / booking confirmation
- Ferry company notice or cancellation message
- Photos/screenshots of delay, app status, emails or SMS
- Receipts for extra costs, if any
- Luggage report or written refusal, if relevant

### Draft Complaint Email
Subject: Claim request for [route/date/booking]

Dear [company],

I am writing regarding [brief issue]. On [date], I travelled / was scheduled to travel on [route]. [Explain facts in 3-5 lines.]

Based on the circumstances, I request [refund / compensation review / written explanation / luggage compensation review]. Attached are the relevant documents.

Please confirm receipt and advise the next step.

Kind regards,
[Name]

### Follow-Up Route
- Send to ferry company support/contact form first.
- If no answer or refusal, use relevant official/consumer complaint route.
- Keep copies of all messages.

## Safety Rules

- Do not ask for booking account passwords, passport scans, card details, bank details or full IDs.
- Do not promise compensation.
- Do not present as lawyer, official authority or ferry-company representative.
- For large losses, injury or complex legal disputes, recommend official consumer/legal help.
- Keep the offer to prep, translation, organization and reminders.

## Decision After Test

- 3+ free checks accepted and 1+ paid-intent reply: keep PROBATION and build one paid pilot.
- Good engagement but zero pay intent: keep as free content/lead magnet, not a paid startup.
- Low lead density again: kill the paid idea; the public lead surface is too thin.
