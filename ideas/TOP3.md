## #1: Social Tourism Availability Caller
**Score:** 6.7 (probation)

**Problem:** Social-tourism voucher holders have the benefit, but finding an actually available provider is a messy phone hunt. Public snippets show people calling many hotels, getting no answers, or hearing there is no availability once they mention the voucher.

**Solution:** A fixed-fee human calling report: the founder calls official registry providers for the buyer's dates/region, verifies live availability and private contribution, then sends 3 realistic options plus exact next steps. No login, no voucher handling, no booking promise.

**Why it is only probation:** Free directories/maps/request tools exist, the buyer is price-sensitive, and today's orthogonal B2B scan did not close the key proof gap. The next run must prove pay intent or kill it.
