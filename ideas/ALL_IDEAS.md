### Ferry Delay Claim Helper - Score: 6.9/10
**What it is:** A small fixed-fee/bilingual admin service that helps Greece ferry passengers check eligibility and file the right refund/compensation complaint after delays, cancellations or ignored refund emails.
**Why Greece specifically:** Greek island travel creates peak-season ferry disruption pain, but passenger rights are split across EU/YNANP/company pages and angry tourists often leave Greece before chasing the paperwork.
**How to start tomorrow:** (1) Build a one-page checker for route, delay length, ticket value, weather/strike exception and company. (2) Reply to public SeaJets/Blue Star/Tripadvisor/Reddit complaints with a free eligibility note and paid filing help. (3) Charge only for prepared claim packs, not legal advice.
**Revenue model:** EUR15-EUR25 claim-pack fee, or 20% of recovered ticket compensation for tourists who prefer success-based help.
**Realistic Month 1 revenue:** EUR150-EUR600 if 10-25 angry passengers convert during July/August ferry disruption season.
**Biggest risk:** Free official forms, consumer associations, booking platforms and ferry companies may satisfy passengers once they know the rule; willingness to pay for small-ticket claims is unproven.
**Competitor landscape:** Official YNANP/Your Europe/EKPIZO pages explain rights, Ferryhopper helps its own customers find alternatives, and ferry companies publish policy pages, but snippets did not surface a dedicated Greece ferry-delay claim-assist service.
**Timing signal:** July peak island travel plus current review/forum refund anger make the pain immediate; the claim value is calculable at roughly 25%-50% of ticket price when conditions qualify.
**Grounded in:** 2026-07-02 orthogonal search showing EU/Greek ferry passenger compensation rules, recent Greek claim-form articles, and SeaJets/Blue Star review/forum snippets about delays, refunds and poor response.
