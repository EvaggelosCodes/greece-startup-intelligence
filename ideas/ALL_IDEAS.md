## 1. 48h Post-Expo Lead Rescue for Small Greek Exhibitors

**Status:** PROBATION
**Score:** 6.2
**One-liner:** A fixed-fee 48-hour cleanup and follow-up sprint for small Greek companies that return from a trade fair with messy badges, business cards, WhatsApp chats, brochures and promised emails but no disciplined follow-up.
**Why now:** Enterprise Greece says Greek companies are participating in 16 international exhibitions in September 2026. A follow-up search found explicit expo-lead pain language: lead follow-up is where many exhibitors lose the most value, exhibitors forget follow-up work after the event, and agencies already educate the market around lead management. That means the pain is real, but also competitive.
**What it sells:** Not export consulting and not lead generation. It sells a tiny execution sprint: transcribe/clean lead list, tag by priority, draft bilingual follow-up emails, prepare LinkedIn/WhatsApp follow-up text, and hand the owner a day-by-day follow-up checklist.
**First sale:** EUR79-EUR149 after an exhibition, or free first cleanup for one exhibitor in exchange for before/after proof.
**Why only probation:** Agencies and B2B lead-management firms already talk about expo follow-up and lead handling. The only possible founder edge is speed, small-ticket execution, and targeting tiny exhibitors too small for a full agency.
**48h gate:** Find 20 Greek exhibitors from current September exhibition lists or LinkedIn posts. Message with a free mini-cleanup offer for 10 leads. Keep only if 3+ reply, 1+ sends a real lead list/sample, or 1+ says EUR79-EUR149 would be reasonable after the free proof. See `ideas/validation/POST_EXPO_LEAD_RESCUE_VALIDATION.md`.
