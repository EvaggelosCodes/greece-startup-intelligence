### Ferry Passenger Claim Pack - Score: 7.1/10
**What it is:** A small fixed-fee/bilingual admin service that helps Greece ferry passengers check eligibility and prepare refund/compensation complaint packs after delays, cancellations, ignored refund emails, or lost/damaged luggage.
**Why Greece specifically:** Greek island travel creates peak-season ferry disruption pain, but passenger rights are split across EU/YNANP/company pages and angry tourists often leave Greece before chasing the paperwork.
**How to start tomorrow:** (1) Build a one-page checker for route, delay length, ticket value, weather/strike exception, luggage receipt and company. (2) Reply to public SeaJets/Blue Star/Tripadvisor/Reddit complaints with a free eligibility note and paid filing pack. (3) Charge only for prepared claim packs and templates, not legal advice.
**Revenue model:** EUR15-EUR35 claim-pack fee, or 20% of recovered compensation for tourists who prefer success-based help.
**Realistic Month 1 revenue:** EUR200-EUR800 if 10-30 angry passengers convert during July/August ferry disruption season, with lost-luggage cases carrying higher upside.
**Biggest risk:** Free official forms, consumer associations, booking platforms and ferry companies may satisfy passengers once they know the rule; willingness to pay for small-ticket delay claims is still unproven.
**Competitor landscape:** Official YNANP/Your Europe/EKPIZO pages explain rights, Ferryhopper helps its own customers find alternatives, ferry companies publish policy pages, and Consumer Ombudsman cases show a free escalation route. Snippets still did not surface a dedicated Greece ferry passenger claim-pack service.
**Timing signal:** July peak island travel plus current review/forum refund anger make the pain immediate; delay claim value is calculable at roughly 25%-50% of ticket price when conditions qualify, while lost-luggage examples show EUR500-EUR740 recoveries.
**Grounded in:** 2026-07-02 searches showing EU/Greek ferry passenger compensation rules, recent Greek claim-form articles, SeaJets/Blue Star review/forum refund frustration, and Consumer Ombudsman/Lawspot sea-travel luggage compensation examples.
