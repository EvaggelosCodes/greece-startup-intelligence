## 1. Specialist Accountant Finder for Online Earners

**Status:** PROBATION
**Score:** 6.4
**One-liner:** A fixed-fee intake and specialist-accountant shortlist for Greeks with nonstandard online income or assets: Amazon KDP, creators/influencers, foreign clients, IBKR/DEGIRO/Revolut, Stripe/PayPal, and platform income.
**Why now:** A fresh July 2026 Reddit post says a KDP seller needs an accountant who knows Amazon because their own accountant cannot handle it and everyone asked had no idea. Other PersonalFinanceGreece snippets repeat the same "my accountant does not know / I cannot find a knowledgeable accountant" pattern around IBKR/DEGIRO/Revolut investments.
**What it sells:** Not tax advice. It sells problem intake, document checklist, accountant-fit questions, and 3-5 verified accountants who explicitly say they handle the niche.
**First sale:** EUR29-EUR49 for a curated shortlist and prep pack, refundable if no accountant accepts the case.
**Why not active yet:** Specialist accounting content already exists for e-shops, digital economy, influencers and online accounting, with online accounting starting around EUR100/month. This could be channel-owned by accountants if the founder cannot source a verified bench.
**48h gate:** Contact/reply to 20 relevant online-earner or investor posts and call/message 15 accountant offices. Keep only if 3+ buyers ask for details and 5+ accountants confirm niche handling. See `ideas/validation/SPECIALIST_ACCOUNTANT_FINDER_VALIDATION.md`.
