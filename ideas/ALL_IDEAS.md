## 1. Specialist Accountant Finder for Online Earners

**Status:** PROBATION
**Score:** 6.7
**One-liner:** A fixed-fee intake and specialist-accountant shortlist for Greeks with nonstandard online income or assets, starting with foreign-broker/ETF investors and expanding later to Amazon KDP, creators/influencers, foreign clients, Stripe/PayPal, and platform income.
**Why now:** A fresh July 2026 Reddit post says a KDP seller needs an accountant who knows Amazon because their own accountant cannot handle it and everyone asked had no idea. Follow-up made the first wedge clearer: IBKR/DEGIRO/Revolut/Trading212/ETF tax reporting has repeated "I cannot find an accountant who knows," "my accountant does not know what this product is," "old-school accountant," and "3 accountants told me different things" language. A later supply check found at least one current Greek accounting office explicitly publishing Crypto & ETF tax guidance and inviting investor cases.
**What it sells:** Not tax/investment advice. It sells problem intake, document checklist, accountant-fit questions, and 3-5 verified accountants who explicitly say they handle the niche. For investor cases, the deliverable is "tax-ready handoff to accountant," not an answer about what to declare.
**First sale:** EUR29-EUR49 for a curated shortlist and prep pack, refundable if no accountant accepts the case.
**Why not active yet:** Specialist accounting content already exists for e-shops, e-commerce/Amazon, digital economy, influencers, online accounting and now crypto/ETF investor tax. This could be channel-owned by accountants if the founder cannot verify 5+ accepting offices and buyers do not pay for routing.
**48h gate:** Contact/reply to 20 relevant foreign-broker/ETF or online-earner posts and call/message 15 accountant offices. Keep only if 3+ buyers ask for details, 5+ accountants confirm niche handling, and 1+ buyer says EUR29-EUR49 is reasonable. See `ideas/validation/SPECIALIST_ACCOUNTANT_FINDER_VALIDATION.md`.
