## 1. Specialist Accountant Finder for Online Earners

**Status:** PROBATION
**Score:** 6.6
**One-liner:** A fixed-fee intake and specialist-accountant shortlist for Greeks with nonstandard online income or assets: Amazon KDP, creators/influencers, foreign clients, IBKR/DEGIRO/Revolut, Stripe/PayPal, and platform income.
**Why now:** A fresh July 2026 Reddit post says a KDP seller needs an accountant who knows Amazon because their own accountant cannot handle it and everyone asked had no idea. A stronger follow-up found several repeat buyer surfaces around IBKR/DEGIRO/Revolut/Trading212/ETF tax reporting: "I cannot find an accountant who knows," "my accountant does not know what this product is," "old-school accountant," and inconsistent advice from multiple accountants.
**What it sells:** Not tax advice. It sells problem intake, document checklist, accountant-fit questions, and 3-5 verified accountants who explicitly say they handle the niche.
**First sale:** EUR29-EUR49 for a curated shortlist and prep pack, refundable if no accountant accepts the case.
**Why not active yet:** Specialist accounting content already exists for e-shops, e-commerce/Amazon, digital economy, influencers and online accounting, with online accounting and ecommerce/Amazon packages starting around EUR100/month. This could be channel-owned by accountants if the founder cannot source a verified bench and buyers do not pay for routing.
**48h gate:** Contact/reply to 20 relevant online-earner or investor posts and call/message 15 accountant offices. Keep only if 3+ buyers ask for details, 5+ accountants confirm niche handling, and 1+ buyer says EUR29-EUR49 is reasonable. See `ideas/validation/SPECIALIST_ACCOUNTANT_FINDER_VALIDATION.md`.
