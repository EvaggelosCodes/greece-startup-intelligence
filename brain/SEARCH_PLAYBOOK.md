# Mike's Search Playbook - how I learn to hunt better
*Read before searching and update after. The point is that run 50 searches better than run 1.*

- 2026-08-24: On long-gap runs, age every probation idea before chasing new snippets. If the 48-hour gate was never passed and fresh search still shows substitutes or official rails, kill the zombie instead of collecting prettier evidence.
- 2026-07-03: For parent-deadline or subsidy windows, pair the buyer query with the application-owner and support-professional terms (`aitisi`, `voucher`, `trapeza`, `michanikos`, `technikos symvoulos`). If centers, banks, gov, or advisors dominate before buyer asks, the money is moving but the wedge is institutionally owned.
- 2026-07-03: For probation ideas, the output of a research pass can be a validation kit, not a verdict. Social Tourism snippets kept showing free-tool pressure, but also clarified the exact offer: confirmed options after phone/contact friction. Next proof must be replies.
- 2026-07-03: For Student Housing, never validate as "paid listings." That rhymes with scams and brokerage. Validate only the judgement layer: area, commute, rent sanity, scam flags, and "best 3 to contact first."
- 2026-07-03: For Ferry Claims, validate only prep/translation/organization, not legal representation or guaranteed compensation. The free eligibility check is the hook; paid intent is for a prepared claim pack, not legal advice.
- 2026-07-03: For beneficiary/benefit-handoff ideas, pain-language is not enough. Pair `voitheia`/`den vrisko` with `tha plirona`/`analamvanei` and the free-tool query (`dorean`, `aitima diathesimotitas`, `logariasmos`) before promoting the idea.
- 2026-07-03: Do not let public search be the whole verdict. If an idea scores 5.8-6.4 but has real pain and a concrete €0 outreach test, keep it as `PROBATION` instead of killing it. The next learning must come from replies, not another snippet.
- 2026-07-03: Pay language is not enough either. `anazito kapoion freelancer na analavei` surfaced real WooCommerce demand, but agencies/platforms dominated immediately. Promotion needs pay language + weak substitute ownership + a reachable channel edge.
- 2026-07-03: One vivid direct ask is not a market. Pet sitting had repeat asks but platform/hotel capture; fishing-lure repair had specificity but weak repeatability. For direct-post niches, require at least repeatable lead density + weak owner layer before scoring.
- 2026-07-03: "Needs a person" is not a wedge. Seasonal staffing, plant/house checks and one-hour interpreter help showed that repeated asks still fail when the job is low-ticket/high-trust or already brokered by job boards, platforms, tenders or professional networks.

## What Works
- Greek-first for laws, prices and complaints; English only for nomad, tourist, diaspora, or app-store/global competitor checks.
- Add the year plus law/deadline words when researching regulation.
- `site:taxheaven.gr` is high-signal for tax/e-invoicing timing.
- Search official deadline source + pricing + social truth for compliance ideas. Example: `site:taxheaven.gr ilektroniki timologisi 2026`, then `myDATA timi`, then reddit/social complaint search.
- For marketplaces, search app names plus local intent. App visibility is a competitor signal even before local market share is proven.

## What Wastes Tokens
- Vague English "Greece business opportunity" searches.
- Fetching pages when snippets already answer timing, price or competitor existence.
- Broad competitor-landscape searches before one exact "already exists?" query.
- Treating a free government compliance tool as irrelevant. It changes the business model.

## Source Signal Ratings
- taxheaven.gr / official tax snippets - HIGH for regulation and timing.
- Greek vendor pricing pages - MED/HIGH for price discovery, but biased toward paid solutions.
- Reddit/social snippets - HIGH for pain language, LOW for market size.
- App stores / global marketplace pages - MED for competitor existence, LOW for Greek supply density unless local listings are visible.
