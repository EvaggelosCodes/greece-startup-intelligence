New rule: repeated asks still fail if the job is either low-value/high-trust or already a brokered skill market.

## 2026-07-03
I ran one more empty-pool category exploration and the filter held. Public-errand/delegation language is too vague: there is anxiety around public services, but not a clean paid "stand in line / handle this" buyer surface, and gov.gr/KEP absorb much of the official-routing job. My new rule is getting sharper: a single direct ask can inspire a search, but active ideas need repeatable lead density plus weak owner layer.

## CURRENT THESIS
The next strong idea needs four things at once: pay language, repeatable lead density, weak substitute ownership, and a reachable first-sale channel. A single vivid direct ask is not enough. A repeated direct ask is not enough if the job is low-value/high-trust or an already-brokered skill market. Money in motion is not enough either if the public surface belongs to the bank, center, engineer, accountant, platform, or official application rail. The founder edge lives where repeated buyers ask for a job and the first response is not already a platform, agency, specialist, hotel, shop, official rail, job board, or professional network.

## MY AGENDA
1. Search for repeated direct asks where the visible surface belongs to the buyer, not the application owner.
2. Add a value/trust gate before scoring: low-ticket home/personal tasks need exceptional channel edge.
3. Re-check one September-side admin window only when buyer-language beats accountant/provider/support pages.

## HUNCHES I CAN'T PROVE YET
- A strong zero-capital winner may look like unglamorous calling, confirming, or screening inside an existing entitlement or budget, not another explanation pack.
- Benefit-admin or subsidy workflows may be safer than tourist-convenience or SME-ops ideas, but only when the user cannot solve the task with a free request/organization layer.
- Direct freelancer-job snippets are better pay proof than complaints, but they may also prove the market is already a commodity channel.
- Hobby and pet-care posts are useful smell tests, but they must show repeatable lead density and weak existing supply before becoming an idea.
- Parent-deadline or subsidy windows may still be good search terrain, but only if parents/homeowners ask for paid outside help rather than just following center, bank, or engineer instructions.
- Brokered skill markets can look like founder opportunities because the ask is explicit, but if procurement, agencies, tenders or job boards already gather supply, the wedge is probably fake.
- September may still reopen boring compliance ideas, but only if buyer-language appears before vendor-owned implementation and support pages.
- I may now overcorrect toward beneficiary workflows, so pay intent needs to stay the first gate, not the last.

## WHAT'S NAGGING ME
The danger now is over-reading "money moving" or "someone needs a person" as progress. The next search must prove not only demand, but why Evaggelos gets trust or supply faster than the existing owner of the workflow.
