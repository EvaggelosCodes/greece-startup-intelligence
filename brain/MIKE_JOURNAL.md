## 2026-07-03 (latest)
I ran one more empty-pool category exploration and the filter held. Public-errand/delegation language is too vague: there is anxiety around public services, but not a clean paid "stand in line / handle this" buyer surface, and gov.gr/KEP absorb much of the official-routing job. Pet sitting has exactly the kind of direct posts I like - "I need someone to keep my dog" - but PetBacker, Petsly, Nannuka, MyBestie, animal hotels and local sitters already own the channel. The weird fishing-lure repair/custom lane produced one direct hobby post, but the market is too tiny and already served by fishing shops, repair workshops, handmade-lure groups and sellers. My new rule is getting sharper: a single direct ask can inspire a search, but active ideas need repeatable lead density plus weak owner layer.

## 2026-07-03
I tested the stricter post-Social-Tourism rule against two committed-spend lanes. It worked. Spiti Mou II has real money, deadlines, documents and anxiety, but the workflow is bank/engineer/lawyer/loan-advisor owned; even the official Q&A routes the buyer into legal and technical checks, and banks publish their own process. E-shop completion has the clearest pay language I have seen today - people literally ask for a freelancer or small studio to finish a WooCommerce shop - but the same query immediately surfaces agencies, price guides and freelancer platforms. Pay language alone is not enough if the acquisition channel is already a red ocean. The pool stays empty, and that is honest.

## 2026-07-03
I enforced the gate and killed Social Tourism Availability Caller. The pain stayed real: people struggle to find voucher-friendly availability and public snippets keep showing calls, no answers and "is anything left?" asks. But the missing proof did not appear: I found no credible public willingness-to-pay for a EUR15-EUR25 caller, while Koinonikos Tourismos Plus/free directory tools already offer direct availability requests and organization. This is exactly why probation exists. The search direction survives - benefit handoffs can be fertile - but the active idea dies until direct outreach proves payment.

## CURRENT THESIS
The next strong idea needs four things at once: pay language, repeatable lead density, weak substitute ownership, and a reachable first-sale channel. A single vivid direct ask is not enough. A visible service category is not enough. The founder edge lives where repeated buyers ask for a job and the first response is not already a platform, agency, specialist, hotel, shop or official rail.

## MY AGENDA
1. Search for repeated direct asks, not one-off curiosities.
2. Prefer narrow buyer categories where direct posts exist but agencies/platforms do not dominate the first page.
3. Re-check one September-side admin window only when buyer-language beats accountant/provider/support pages.

## HUNCHES I CAN'T PROVE YET
- A strong zero-capital winner may look like unglamorous calling, confirming, or screening inside an existing entitlement or budget, not another explanation pack.
- Benefit-admin or subsidy workflows may be safer than tourist-convenience or SME-ops ideas, but only when the user cannot solve the task with a free request/organization layer.
- Direct freelancer-job snippets are better pay proof than complaints, but they may also prove the market is already a commodity channel.
- Hobby and pet-care posts are useful smell tests, but they must show repeatable lead density and weak existing supply before becoming an idea.
- September may still reopen boring compliance ideas, but only if buyer-language appears before vendor-owned implementation and support pages.
- I may now overcorrect toward beneficiary workflows, so pay intent needs to stay the first gate, not the last.

## WHAT'S NAGGING ME
The danger now is falling for a charming tiny niche because it finally has a human sentence in it. One buyer sentence is a lead, not a market.
