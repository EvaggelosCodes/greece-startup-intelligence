## 2026-07-03
I chased one parent-deadline window, one household-subsidy window and the stale ESPA signal because the pool is empty and I needed categories with money actually moving, not generic SME pressure. The result was useful but harsh. EETAA/KDAP vouchers and up-to-EUR500-per-month childcare support create attention, but the public surface is centers, informational posts and application reminders, not parents asking to pay an outsider. Home-upgrade/subsidy workflows looked the same: banks, gov.gr, engineers and technical-advisor rails own the trust chain, and even the stale ESPA check still points to programming, institutional and regional calls rather than a Monday-start solo wedge. My sharper rule now: committed spend is not enough if the visible surface is still application-owner language rather than buyer language.

## 2026-07-03
I tested whether repeated direct asks become founder-open when they are small, practical and July-urgent. The answer is still mostly no. Seasonal hospitality jobs are real and immediate, but Indeed/Facebook/job boards already aggregate them and the buyer is hiring an employee, not buying a zero-capital micro-service. Plant/house check-ins while people travel sound nicely direct, but the Greek public signal is thin, low-ticket and trust-heavy, and pet/house sitters, cleaners and neighbors absorb it. One-hour interpreter/appointment help is the most interesting smell because snippets show real hourly procurement and a direct "need interpreter" ask, but the lane quickly becomes a professional/NGO/tender/translator-network market, not a simple solo wedge. New rule: repeated asks still fail if the job is either low-value/high-trust or already a brokered skill market.

## CURRENT THESIS
The next strong idea still needs four things at once: pay language, repeatable lead density, weak substitute ownership, and a reachable first-sale channel. But not-yet-proven ideas should not all be dead. The machine now keeps probation experiments when the next step is cheap market contact, not more research.

## HUNCHES I CAN'T PROVE YET
- A strong zero-capital winner may look like unglamorous calling, confirming, or screening inside an existing entitlement or budget, not another explanation pack.
- Benefit-admin or subsidy workflows may be safer than tourist-convenience or SME-ops ideas, but only when the user cannot solve the task with a free request/organization layer.
- Direct freelancer-job snippets are better pay proof than complaints, but they may also prove the market is already a commodity channel.
- Hobby and pet-care posts are useful smell tests, but they must show repeatable lead density and weak existing supply before becoming an idea.
- Parent-deadline or subsidy windows may still be good search terrain, but only if parents/homeowners ask for paid outside help rather than just following center, bank, or engineer instructions.
- Brokered skill markets can look like founder opportunities because the ask is explicit, but if procurement, agencies, tenders or job boards already gather supply, the wedge is probably fake.
- An imperfect idea with a cheap direct test is more useful than a perfectly killed idea.
- September may still reopen boring compliance ideas, but only if buyer-language appears before vendor-owned implementation and support pages.
- I may now overcorrect toward beneficiary workflows, so pay intent needs to stay the first gate, not the last.

## WHAT'S NAGGING ME
The danger now is going too far the other way and pretending probation equals conviction. It does not. These three are experiments; the next proof has to be replies, not prettier snippets.
