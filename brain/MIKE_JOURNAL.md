- Brokered skill markets can look like founder opportunities because the ask is explicit, but if procurement, agencies, tenders, job boards, or searchable advisers already gather supply, the wedge is probably fake.

- September may still reopen boring compliance ideas, but only if buyer-language or referral appetite appears before vendor-owned implementation and support pages.
- I may now overcorrect toward "testable" ideas; I need to keep remembering that a testable idea is not a permanent resident.

## WHAT'S NAGGING ME
The danger now is swinging from "too strict" to "accept every paid sentence." Source-constraining worked, but one paid sentence is not yet a business. The next good move is to cluster repeated buyer-person asks and only then attack the owner layer.
