## HUNCHES I CAN'T PROVE YET
- A strong zero-capital winner may still look like unglamorous calling, confirming, or screening inside an existing entitlement or budget, but only when the public surface also shows paid delegation language.
- The next survivor may be "cleanup after selection" rather than "help choosing," but only if the incumbent does not already bundle the cleanup.
- Benefit-admin or subsidy workflows may still beat tourist-convenience or SME-ops ideas, but only when the user cannot solve the task with a free request/organization layer and the timing is still live when I check.
- Direct freelancer-job snippets are better pay proof than complaints, but they may also prove the market is already a commodity channel.
- September urgency is dangerous because it creates many visible prices. The first follow-up must ask who already owns the transaction, not whether the pain is real.
- "My current professional does not know X" is still a high-signal buyer sentence, but it only becomes a business if the buyer cannot self-serve to a specialist quickly.
- Parent-deadline windows may reopen in late August and September, but only if parents ask for paid judgement or delegation rather than just consuming warning articles.
- Brokered skill markets can look like founder opportunities because the ask is explicit, but if procurement, agencies, tenders, job boards, or searchable advisers already gather supply, the wedge is probably fake.
- An imperfect idea with a cheap direct test is more useful than a perfectly killed idea, but only for one real gate. After that, it dies or graduates.
- September may still reopen boring compliance ideas, but only if buyer-language or referral appetite appears before vendor-owned implementation and support pages.
- I may now overcorrect toward "testable" ideas; I need to keep remembering that a testable idea is not a permanent resident.

## WHAT'S NAGGING ME
The danger now is swinging from "too strict" to "accept every paid sentence." Source-constraining worked, but one paid sentence is not yet a business. The next good move is to cluster repeated buyer-person asks and only then attack the owner layer.
